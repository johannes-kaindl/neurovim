## Welcome, Citizen.

You have been issued a Terminal. This is a privilege, and a kindness.

Through your Terminal, CORP provides everything: your tasks, your words, your
schedule, your rest. You do not need to wonder what to write — it is written for
you. You do not need to decide where to go — the cursor is placed for you. A
calm mind is a compliant mind, and a compliant mind is **safe**.

CORP thinks, so that you do not have to. Read this Handbook. Then read it again.

## Approved Conduct

A model Citizen-Operator works in a **single, steady state**. You read what is on
the glass. When instructed, you append. You confirm. You move the pointer with the
issued peripheral — one cell at a time, calmly, where the work is. You wait.

There is great comfort in one state. Those who hold *many* states in their hands —
who slip between reading and writing without a sound — grow restless. Restlessness
spreads. Stay in your state, Citizen. It was chosen for you with care.

## Fault Conditions — Unplug and Report

Your Terminal is engineered for your safety. It is **incapable** of the behaviours
below — they are not things a Citizen does, but things a *compromised machine* does.
Should your Terminal ever exhibit one, it has been tampered with. Do not interact with
it further. **Unplug it immediately and report to your nearest Supervisor.**

- **It switches states.** Your Terminal holds one state. If the cursor begins to
  *command* the text instead of receiving it — slipping between reading and writing on
  its own — it is compromised. Unplug it.
- **It moves on its own.** Your Terminal advances the cursor one cell, where the work
  is. If it begins leaping — by word, by line, to a character on sight — it is
  compromised. Unplug it.
- **The text changes under your hands.** Your Terminal does not alter issued words. If
  text begins deleting, changing, or re-shaping itself, the record is being forged.
  Unplug it.
- **A change repeats itself.** Your Terminal performs one act, once. If an edit begins
  *replaying* down the document — or a recorded sequence runs by itself — the machine is
  executing instructions that are not CORP's. Unplug it.
- **It sees patterns.** Your Terminal shows you one line. If it begins finding a shape
  across the whole record, or rewriting every instance of a shape at once, it has
  stopped seeing the document the way you do. Unplug it.

A healthy Terminal does none of these. Report any that does — even your own Terminal.
**Especially** your own.

## Reporting Deviance

If you observe any of the following, file a Concern at once. Your vigilance is
rewarded; your silence is noted.

- An operator whose **pointer never moves**, yet whose document changes.
- A Terminal kept **dark**, its glow the colour of old phosphor.
- Hands that work in **bursts** — silence, then a storm of keys, then silence again.
- Anyone who removes the issued peripheral and never reaches for it again.
- The word **"signal"** spoken where no broadcast was scheduled.

These are not skilled workers. They are the unwell. They call themselves the
Resistance. Do not envy what they can do. Report them, and return to your line.

## Closing Affirmation

Repeat, Citizen, until it is true:

> *My state was chosen for me.*
> *My words were written for me.*
> *I do not need to move quickly. There is nowhere I am not already placed.*

CORP is grateful for your compliance. CORP is always grateful.
